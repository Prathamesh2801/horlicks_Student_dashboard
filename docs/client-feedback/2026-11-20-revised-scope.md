@Gaurav  Sir Zeal Interactive bhai @Sanskriti Zeal Revised scope
Access and login
•    One shared login ID and password for the staff.
•    Staff select their name at the start of each visit (a dropdown rather than free text avoids spelling variants).
•    Separate admin and client logins for the dashboards.
Staff app
•    Visit details: date, city, school, staff name.
•    Offline mode: visits are saved on the phone and synced later in low network zone.
Admin dashboard
•    Targets vs achievement:
o    progress bars, run-rate, required per day and projected finish date
•    Cards and charts:
o    average students per visit and per school
•    Filters on every screen (date range, city, staff, status), carried into exports.
•    Exports: Excel, CSV, PDF and a one-click campaign report or PPT with charts and sample photos.
Client dashboard (read-only)
•    Top KPIs: students reached, schools covered, visits completed, % of target.
•    Target vs achievement by city, with a progress bar and projected finish.
•    Trend chart over time and city-wise bar chart.
•    Average students per visit and per school.
•    Map of covered schools.
•    Photo gallery: selected best activity photos only
•    Date and city filters, and download of the summary report (PDF or PPT).
•    Hidden from the client: user management, staff-level data, drafts, sync issues and AI debugging.
Things to watch
•    With a shared login, accountability depends on the staff name, so keep the dropdown and the GPS and time stamps.
•    Show the client "last updated" and only Completed, verified visits, so numbers don't change under them. Request you to please share the revised UI by tomorrow with brand logo and city list.
